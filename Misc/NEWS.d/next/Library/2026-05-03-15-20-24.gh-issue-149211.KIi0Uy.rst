The C ``_warnings`` module now caches the Python-level
``_showwarnmsg`` callable and ``WarningMessage`` class so that warnings
emitted after the ``warnings`` module has begun being torn down (for
example, from a ``__del__`` during interpreter shutdown) can still
dispatch through a user-installed :func:`warnings.showwarning` hook,
provided at least one prior warning emission has primed the cache.
